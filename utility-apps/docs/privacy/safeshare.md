# Privacy policy – SafeShare ID

*Last updated: 17 November 2026*

This policy covers the Android app **SafeShare ID** (`io.github.sachin102492.safeshare`), made by Sachin (contact: princendlesster@gmail.com).

## What the app does with your data
- **Photos you choose or share into the app** are processed entirely on your phone, using Google's on-device ML Kit text and QR readers (provided through Google Play services). The images are not uploaded to us or to Google for processing.
- The protected copy is written to the app's private cache only so you can send it, and is deleted the next time you protect a photo or open the app. If you tap *Save copy*, it is saved to Pictures/SafeShare on your phone. Location data (EXIF) from the original photo is not copied.
- **The 'Who has a copy' list** (purpose and date) is stored on your phone only.

We do not run any server for this app, and we do not collect, sell or receive your personal data.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
The app shows ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share:
- your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
- approximate location (derived from your IP address),
- app interactions and diagnostic/crash information.

This data is encrypted in transit. Google's use is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads). Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads, and you can change your choice any time from **About › Ad privacy choices**.

## Permissions
None. Photos are chosen through Android's photo picker, which needs no storage permission.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes and contact
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date. Questions or deletion requests: princendlesster@gmail.com. Because your data stays on your phone, uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.
