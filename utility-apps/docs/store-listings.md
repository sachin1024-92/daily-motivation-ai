## Seeti
**Title:** Seeti: Cooker Whistle Counter
**Short description:** Counts your pressure cooker's whistles and rings when the dal is done.

**Full description:**
Stop standing in the kitchen counting whistles.

Set how many whistles your dish needs (dal 3, rice 2, rajma 6…), tap Start, and walk away. Seeti listens for your pressure cooker, counts every whistle, and rings an alarm the moment the count is reached, even with the screen off.

• Counts whistles automatically, ignores talking, TV and short beeps
• Presets for rice, dal, aloo, chana, rajma and mutton
• Low / Medium / High sensitivity for noisy kitchens or another room
• Safety alarm after 15–45 minutes, in case a whistle is missed
• +1 / −1 buttons to correct the count
• Works offline

Privacy: the microphone is used only while you are counting. Sound is checked on your phone and is never recorded, saved or uploaded.

## Bijli Guard
**Title:** Bijli Guard: Bill Predictor
**Short description:** Predicts your electricity bill and warns before you cross 150 units.

**Full description:**
Know your electricity bill before it arrives.

Enter the reading from your last bill, then read your meter every few days. Bijli Guard works out how many units you are using a day, predicts this month's units and bill, and warns you before you cross a costlier slab.

For Madhya Pradesh: crossing 150 units in a month loses the Atal Griha Jyoti subsidy (₹1 per unit for the first 100 units). Bijli Guard tells you the date you'll cross it, how much extra it would cost, and how many units a day keep you under.

For landlords with sub-meters: add each tenant's previous and current reading, set your rate per unit, and send each tenant their bill on WhatsApp in one tap.

• Slab-wise bill estimate with fuel surcharge and electricity duty
• MP 2026-27 domestic rates built in; every rate is editable for other states
• Tenant sub-meter bills, shared on WhatsApp
• All data stays on your phone

Estimates only. Your electricity company's bill is final. Not affiliated with any government body or electricity company.

## SafeShare ID
**Title:** SafeShare ID: Mask & Watermark
**Short description:** Hide your ID number and stamp who it's for before sharing an ID photo.

**Full description:**
Hotels, offices, landlords and apps keep asking for a photo of your ID. SafeShare makes the copy you send useless for anything else.

1. Pick a photo of your ID card (or share it into SafeShare from the gallery or WhatsApp).
2. SafeShare finds the 12-digit ID number and hides the first 8 digits, the same as a masked Aadhaar, and hides the QR code.
3. Type who the copy is for, e.g. "Hotel Raj check-in". That and today's date are stamped across the photo.
4. Send it, or save it.

• Automatic detection of 12-digit ID numbers, 16-digit Virtual IDs and QR codes
• Hide anything else by dragging a box over it
• Removes location data from the photo
• Keeps a private list of who has a copy
• Works on your phone: photos are never uploaded

Always check the copy before sending; automatic detection can miss things. Not affiliated with UIDAI or any government body.

## Ghar Clock
**Title:** Ghar Clock: LPG & Home Alerts
**Short description:** Predicts when your LPG cylinder runs out; reminders for RO, AC and inverter.

**Full description:**
Never run out of gas again.

Ghar Clock learns how long your LPG cylinder lasts from your own refill dates and reminds you to book a refill a few days before it runs out. It also keeps track of everything else in the house that needs regular attention:

• RO / water purifier service
• AC service before summer
• Inverter battery water top-up
• Kitchen chimney filter cleaning
• Water tank cleaning, pest control
• Gas pipe (Suraksha hose) replacement, fire extinguisher refill
• Bike / car service and PUC certificate
• Or add your own

Tap "Done today" when it's done. Save your gas agency or service person's number to call or WhatsApp them in one tap. A daily morning reminder lists anything due soon.

All your data stays on your phone.
