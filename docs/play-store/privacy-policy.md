# Orbit privacy policy

_Last updated: 6 November 2026_

Orbit ("the app") is published on Google Play by princendlesster. This policy explains what the app does with information. Questions: **princendlesster@gmail.com**.

## Summary

- Orbit has no user accounts and no Orbit servers. Your profile, linked handles, habits and settings are stored **only on your device**.
- The creators, posts, channels and chat replies shown in the app are sample content. Messages you write in the preview stay on your device.
- If ads are shown, Google AdMob may collect device identifiers to show and measure ads. Orbit Plus subscribers see no ads.
- Purchases are handled by Google Play. Orbit never sees your payment details.

## Information stored on your device

Orbit saves the following with Android's app storage so it survives restarts: your display name, username and bio; social handles you choose to link; habits and streaks; theme choice; whether you have Orbit Plus; and how many free Orbit AI messages you used today. Posts, chats and service listings you create in the preview are kept in memory and cleared when the app restarts.

You can erase everything at any time in **Profile → Settings → Delete my data**, or by uninstalling the app.

## Orbit AI

Orbit AI answers on your device with built-in responses, and the Play Store version sends your Orbit AI messages nowhere. If a future version connects Orbit AI to an online model such as xAI's Grok, the text you send to Orbit AI will be sent to that provider to generate a reply, and this policy will be updated before that version is released.

## Advertising

Free users may see ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (used for approximate location), app interactions and diagnostics, as described in [Google's policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads, and you can change it later. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

## Purchases

Orbit Plus is sold as a Google Play subscription. Google processes payment under the [Google Payments privacy notice](https://payments.google.com/payments/apis-secure/get_legal_document?ldo=0&ldt=privacynotice). Orbit receives only a purchase confirmation from Google Play to unlock Plus features.

## Sharing and links

When you share content to other apps, Android's share sheet passes it to the app you choose. When you open a linked profile, it opens in that app or your browser. Those apps' own privacy policies apply.

## Children

Orbit is not directed to children under 13 and is listed for adults.

## Changes

We will update this page when the app's data practices change and show the new date above.
