## Full description (max 4000)

```
Orbit brings the ways you share, watch and talk into one app, and it tells you when you're done.

SHARE
• Post photos-style "vibes", text and polls to your feed
• Six reactions, comments and saves
• Stories that disappear, with replies that go straight to chat
• Publish once to your feed, story, reels and your own channel

WATCH
• Short vertical reels: double-tap to like, tap to pause
• Long-form videos with categories, Watch later and Up next

CHAT
• One inbox for 1:1 chats, groups and broadcast channels
• Read receipts, replies, reactions, pinned and muted chats
• Smart reply suggestions
• Orbit AI, your coach for motivation, daily plans and caption ideas

BOOK SERVICES
• Find coaches, photographers, tutors, cooks and more
• Request a service in two taps and agree the details in chat
• Offer your own services and get discovered

STAY WELL
• A daily spark quote, habit streaks and a focus timer
• The feed ends with "You're all caught up" instead of scrolling forever

ORBIT PLUS (optional subscription)
• No ads
• Unlimited Orbit AI messages (free: 15 per day)
• Premium vibes and a Plus badge
• Featured placement for your service listings

Orbit is in preview: the creators, posts, channels and chat replies you meet are sample content so you can try every feature. Your profile and habits stay on your device.
```

> Keep other companies' app names out of the listing. Play's metadata policy treats them as misleading keywords.
