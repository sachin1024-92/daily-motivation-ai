FloatNote puts a notepad bubble on top of every app. Tap it, type, and tap away. Every keystroke is saved automatically.

<b>Floats over everything</b>
• Chat-head style bubble you can drag anywhere
• Fling it and it springs to the nearest edge with physics-based motion
• When idle it tucks itself half off-screen and fades, out of your way
• Drag it onto the ✕ target (it snaps in magnetically) to hide it

<b>Autosave, always</b>
• No save button: notes save as you type
• Switch between notes, start a new one, or copy a note from the bubble
• Pop any note out of the app into the floating notepad

<b>Quick access</b>
• Quick Settings tile to toggle the bubble
• Notification shortcut to open your note

<b>Private by design</b>
• Your notes never leave your device, and there is no account
• One small banner ad on the notes list, never in the bubble or while you type
• Free and open source (MIT)

<b>Support the project (optional)</b>
• Every feature is free. Leave a one-time tip of any size to remove the ad for good and unlock extra bubble colours
