# FloatNote Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 13 November 2026_

FloatNote is a free, open-source notepad. There are two builds, and they differ in one way:
the Google Play build shows an ad.

## Both builds

- **Your notes stay on your device.** Notes are stored in the app's private storage and are
  never sent to the developer, to Google's advertising services or to anyone else. They are
  included in Android's own device backup only if you have enabled it in system settings.
- **No account, and no analytics of our own.** The developer collects nothing.
- **Permissions.**
  - *Display over other apps*: used only to draw the floating bubble and notepad you turn on.
  - *Notifications / foreground service*: shows the ongoing notification required by Android
    while the bubble is on screen, with shortcuts to open or hide it.

## Google Play build

- **Advertising.** The notes list shows one banner ad served by Google AdMob. To do this the
  app includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK and uses the Internet permission. Google may collect
  and use your device's advertising ID, IP address (which gives an approximate location),
  device information and how you interact with the ad, to show and measure ads. See
  [how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
  The content of your notes is not read or sent.
- **Your choices.** In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before loading
  ads. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google →
  Ads. Any supporter tip removes the ad permanently, and the ad SDK is then not started.
- **Optional tips.** Supporter tips are processed entirely by Google Play under Google's
  privacy policy. FloatNote never sees your payment details. It only stores, on your device,
  whether you are a supporter so it can remove the ad and unlock bubble colours.

## FOSS build (GitHub / F-Droid)

- **No ads and no Internet permission.** This build contains no advertising or Google code
  and cannot send anything off your device.
