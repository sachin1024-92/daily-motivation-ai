# FloatNote Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 6 November 2026_

FloatNote is a free, open-source notepad. It is built to collect nothing.

- **No data collection.** FloatNote does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal
  or usage data. The app does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send anything
  off your device.
- **Your notes stay on your device.** Notes are stored in the app's private storage. They
  are included in Android's own device backup only if you have enabled it in system settings.
- **Optional tips.** Supporter tips in the Google Play version are processed entirely by
  Google Play under Google's privacy policy. FloatNote never sees your payment details. It
  only stores, on your device, whether you are a supporter so it can unlock bubble colours.
- **No ads, analytics, or trackers.** FloatNote contains no third-party SDKs of this kind.
- **Permissions.**
  - *Display over other apps* — used only to draw the floating bubble and notepad you turn on.
  - *Notifications / foreground service* — shows the ongoing notification required by Android
    while the bubble is on screen, with shortcuts to open or hide it.
